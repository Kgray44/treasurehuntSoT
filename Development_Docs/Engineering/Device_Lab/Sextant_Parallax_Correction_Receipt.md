## Gesture acquisition contract

All six kinds require delivery throttling at most 100 ms, observation age at most 500 ms, observation gaps at most 500 ms, and channel skew at most 100 ms. The quiet stability classifier requires 600 ms continuous observation; configured gesture dwell is 100–10,000 ms and timeout is 100–60,000 ms. Acquisition rejects a lease shorter than stability dwell plus gesture dwell. A caller explicitly choosing a burst must retain the existing 30-second maximum. Actual browser events can be sparse; delivery intervals are not sensor capture-rate promises. Sparse, stale, missing, disturbed or unknown observations produce reset/unavailable behavior and the authored alternative.

| SLOW_SWEEP      | gravity, linear acceleration, angular velocity, stability | DEVICE, configured coverage/speed/dwell                                                                 |

Provider/provenance/frame discontinuity invalidates the assembled channels. Partial acquisition, abort, any ended lease, pause/background and expiration release every owned lease; reacquisition is explicit. Completion callbacks occur once per acquired session. Landfall's legacy 1 Hz contextual hints retain their own accepted meaning and never become a device-gesture acquisition class.

## Native scene boundary v1

START binds a fresh random local session ID, positive epoch, immutable published version checksum and in-run instance ID. A companion advertises scene-transfer version 1; older companions retain Guided View. PLACE, STOP, transfer messages and native tracking/interaction events are scoped to that session/epoch. A stale stop cannot terminate a newer provider.

BEGIN declares a unique transaction, monotonic generation, exact total UTF-8 byte count, exact chunk count and SHA-256 digest. CHUNK carries canonical base64 for the next contiguous byte segment. COMMIT requires every chunk, byte count, digest, strict UTF-8, resolved scene identity, coordinate frame and entity validation. A rejected/replayed/missing/reordered/corrupt transaction cannot replace the current scene. A rejected BEGIN cannot overwrite a pending transaction; generation is consumed even on abort. ABORT matches the current transaction only. A 15-second monotonic native deadline clears uncommitted bytes; stop/background/failure clears them immediately. JS bounds individual acknowledgments to five seconds and the transfer to 15 seconds. No camera image, mesh, captured room geometry or world map crosses this protocol.

The existing Drydock canonical presentation limit remains 32 KiB. The schema's per-entity maximum is not a promise that all 32 maximal texts fit that publication capsule. Eight 2,000-character ASCII entities demonstrate accepted content exceeding the old single-request limit. The transport also independently supports 32 maximal ASCII and multibyte texts. Provider safety preflight rejects unsafe composed geometry before transfer and returns to Guided View; publication support does not guarantee usable physical tracking. Android stages all anchors/textures before replacement and releases staged allocations on failure; iOS stages all nodes before replacement. A provider failure terminates truthfully instead of leaving native-ready controls.
