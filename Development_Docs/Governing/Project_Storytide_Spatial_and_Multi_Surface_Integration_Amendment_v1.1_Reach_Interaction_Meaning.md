# Project Storytide Spatial Integration v1.1 — Reach Interaction Meaning

## 1. Authority

This extends the scope-limited [Storytide spatial integration v1.0](Project_Storytide_Spatial_and_Multi_Surface_Integration_Amendment_v1.0.md). It is not a replacement for the future full Storytide governing document. That document must incorporate this integration chain explicitly.

## 2. Narrative versus input

Reach determines calibrated pointing, selection and manipulation intent. Storytide determines what an accepted owner-domain outcome means in the Chronicle. A map glowing on hover, a compass rotating in an inspection view, or an artifact moving between surfaces does not automatically complete a story step.

Storytide consumes approved operation/outcome references, not private hand footage or model landmarks. It may propose completion only through governed canonical runtime logic. One Voyage accepts progression. Reach cannot emit an event whose mere existence advances the Chronicle.

## 3. Authoring model

A Reach-backed moment declares target semantic identity/version, permitted operations, domain outcome requirement, visibility, operation constraints, preferred modality, conventional alternative, cancellation/retry policy, evidence class and interaction-definition version. Creators select approved operations and narrative styling; they cannot tune raw vision confidence, inject executable handlers, bypass confirmation or require a native companion for a browser-baseline mechanic.

A sealed letter may require Crossdeck's acknowledged receipt on an authorized surface. A compass puzzle may require a Parallax-owned constrained transform result. The underlying Reach selection event alone satisfies neither. Public content contains definitions, never private calibration/footage.

## 4. Fallback and accessibility

Every essential moment has an equivalent conventional path. Fallback completion has the same intended story outcome while honestly labeling its evidence class. A keyboard operation is not camera proof, and a simulated ritual is not a physical measurement. Storytide must not punish permission refusal, limited mobility or unsupported browsers with a broken narrative.

Guides explain available operations in context and avoid an arbitrary gesture dictionary. Dwell and two-hand mechanics remain optional unless their alternatives preserve essential outcomes. Reduced motion must keep the narrative state and confirmation understandable.

## 5. Replay and editions

Published definitions bind semantics/version. Replays do not reuse stale Reach sessions or replay an activation event into current authority. Cosmetic inspection transforms may reset or persist under explicit owner policy; durable story actions use canonical receipts. A later model upgrade cannot reinterpret an old gesture puzzle without compatibility review.

## 6. Validation and acceptance

Drydock tests reachable alternatives, unknown operation rejection, owner-domain completion boundaries, false/missing gestures, cancellation, replay, unsupported capability and edition compatibility. Available Storytide integration is required for a live acceptance claim; synthetic narrative adapters are contract evidence only. Narrative feedback follows actual domain outcome, never speculative hand recognition.

## Publication and implementation boundary

This amendment is additive. Preserve the accepted baseline and all historical evidence. Its requirements become the effective documentation contract on protected-main acceptance; they do not claim new runtime implementation or passing physical qualification. Future work uses focused validation, the ordinary Sounding Line final check, protected-main integration, and a landed-tree smoke check. Sounding Line remains the software verification authority; One Voyage remains the progression authority.

The [Reach governing document](Project_Reach_Spatial_Interaction_and_Intent_System_Governing_Document_v1.0.md) defines the shared interaction contracts. The [effective spatial authority index](Voyagewright_Spatial_Experience_Effective_Authority.md) identifies the complete governing chain. Product implementation status remains explicit in the registries and project homes.
