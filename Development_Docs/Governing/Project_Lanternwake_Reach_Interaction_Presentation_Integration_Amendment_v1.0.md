# Project Lanternwake Reach Interaction Presentation — Integration Amendment v1.0

## Authority and scope

This scope-limited integration amendment adds Reach feedback to the existing Lanternwake presentation domain and Spatial Experience architecture. It does not replace Lanternwake's governing history, add a general input engine, or change accepted runtime implementation. It must be incorporated into future consolidated Lanternwake authority.

## State-driven presentation

Reach owns highlight/selection/manipulation intent state; domain owners determine accepted outcomes. Lanternwake styles reticles, target glow, lift, bounded follow motion, pending transfer, cancellation return, accepted landing and optional audio/haptics. Styling cannot manufacture successful selection, commit or acknowledgment.

Highlight must remain stable and restrained while uncertainty is bounded. Ambiguity is communicated rather than hidden behind a confident glow. Selected, holding, interrupted, pending, accepted and rejected states have distinguishable visual/text feedback. A hand disappearing does not trigger a celebratory release effect.

Crossdeck's transaction acknowledgment controls source departure and destination success. Parallax's constrained transform controls spatial presentation. Predicted interpolation is cosmetic and cannot activate or complete a mechanic. Material inertia remains bounded and cannot generate progression.

## Accessibility and performance

Reduced motion replaces large lift/travel/inertia with clear state transitions. Sound and haptics are optional; every meaningful result has a visual/text equivalent. Guidance describes the target's current supported operations without requiring a secret gesture vocabulary.

Rendering may interpolate between fresh measurements, but inference lag or unavailable tracking must become visible degraded/interrupted state. Preserve input responsiveness and avoid cinematic effects that conceal pending authorization or prevent conventional controls. Test on qualified browser configurations and record performance separately from model inference.

## Acceptance

Future integration tests verify state-to-effect mapping, no timer-driven success, cancellation after loss/rejection, reduced motion, contrast, non-audio alternatives, and Crossdeck acknowledgment ordering. Owner review examines both the quality of the illusion and the truth of its state. No live effect integration is claimed by this amendment.

The [Reach constitution](Project_Reach_Spatial_Interaction_and_Intent_System_Governing_Document_v1.0.md) and [effective spatial authority](Voyagewright_Spatial_Experience_Effective_Authority.md) govern the associated input and ownership contract.
