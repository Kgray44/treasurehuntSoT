1. Sign in with the owner account and open **Chronicle Passport**.
2. Use the visible **History** control, open **Your Voyages**, and choose a
   rich Voyage using **Open Voyage**.
3. Confirm the pinned edition, historical Creator and Captain, historical
   crew, completed chapters, safe objective and choice context, artifacts,
   assembly, achievements, and bounded technical provenance.
4. Add and revise a private Reflection and Memory. Confirm these additions do
   not change the Journey Summary or historical facts.
5. Inspect private Memory media. Clean owner-authorized media may open through
   an opaque owner-only route; unavailable, unscanned, withdrawn, archived, or
   revoked media remains unavailable without storage detail.
6. Prepare or refresh a private Keepsake. A participant may set only their own
   consent. Confirm denial excludes representation, grant permits it, and
   revocation removes it after refresh while the underlying Voyage remains the
   same.
7. Use **See what changed** only when the Tideglass-owned comparison handoff is
   present, then return to the Voyage archive.
8. Repeat the page at a narrow viewport, with keyboard navigation, reduced
   motion, and effective 200% zoom. Content must remain readable and usable.
