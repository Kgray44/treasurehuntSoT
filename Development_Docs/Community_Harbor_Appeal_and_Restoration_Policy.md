# Community Harbor Appeal and Restoration Policy

Eligible actions permit one active appeal per action and appellant within a 30-day filing window. Appeal states are `SUBMITTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `INFORMATION_REQUESTED`, `UPHELD`, `OVERTURNED`, `PARTIALLY_OVERTURNED`, `WITHDRAWN`, and `CLOSED`.

Restoration is not a status flip. It requires approved review plus current object checksum, trusted current scanner evidence, package integrity, visibility, consent, location, license, ownership, publication, and sanction checks. Restoration emits an immutable receipt and only then permits reindexing.
