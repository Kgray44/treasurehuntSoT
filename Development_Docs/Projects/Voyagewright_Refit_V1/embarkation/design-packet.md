## Architecture and preservation

The existing Lanternwake motion authority and scene-host ownership contain one
local director. WebGL2 renders perspective, subdivided material deformation,
normal-sensitive lighting, depth attenuation, near defocus and shutter trails.

metal, rope and atmosphere. The Voyage/version seed controls every emitter.

captures are ephemeral browser memory and never uploaded or persisted.

frequencies avoid a short obvious loop. The camera, crop, furniture and UI remain
