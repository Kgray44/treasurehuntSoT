## B. Architecture and first causal failures

The current pipeline remains Sextant camera permission/capabilities/lifecycle → distinct presented frame → bounded one-in-flight current-image neural inference → Watchglass observation/source/anatomy → selected-index appearance correspondence → source/temporal identity → bounded current-position fusion → fresh readiness → calibration sample window → independent held-out validation → display/playground. Hardware discovery remains Sextant's responsibility. No new IR/depth driver or provider is installed.

The image tracker is a local normalized cross-correlation matcher, not a second neural index detector. It uses stable/adaptive grayscale material patches, bounded previous/predicted search proposals, texture and structure checks, competitor separation and actual forward/backward correspondence. It can follow a previously selected fingertip's appearance; it cannot independently infer that an isolated skin patch is the index. Full-hand observations retain semantic/contrary-gesture authority. Compatible current model/image positions already receive a bounded weighted correction. Unverified proposals, elapsed time and model agreement alone cannot create selected-index identity or fresh coordinates.

A new instrumented pass exactly reproduces all 1,336 baseline output/readiness rows on immutable neural packets and grayscale planes with their original capture/completion clocks. Its sixteen-stage waterfall distinguishes frame delivery, inference availability, observation/context integrity, active-hand continuity, selected-index proof, image correspondence, model/image compatibility, position eligibility, readiness and controller admission. The original cached graph does not expose internal palm-detector decisions: those stages remain explicitly unavailable. Semantic correctness is available only at sparse reviewed development references, not every requested prompt frame.

RIGHT first loses position at source time 25.795 seconds after thirteen usable frames. Stable/adaptive image matches still agree near the visible terminal surface, while the neural endpoint is displaced by about fifteen analysis pixels. This clears coordinate registration. All 89 RIGHT packets are fresh, all contain a neural hand, and 88 have a full-point anchor. There are then 75 appearance failures and 76 conflict frames; five old-bank proof successes never lead to optical registration. This is not camera permission failure or a generic stale-packet problem.

BOTTOM-RIGHT starts with twelve tracked frames, loses appearance during a pose change at 42.729 seconds, briefly receives a full-model rescue at 42.845, and loses current coordinate qualification at 42.912. After continued failures, its established image reference expires at 44.079. Later model presence cannot prove the old selected fingertip, and some reviewed anatomy is ambiguous. Widening a timer would not repair that endpoint evidence.

The original broad mixed-motion browser assertion also remains a real failure. Its model source becomes ambiguous during a turn; the true tip then leaves both bounded search proposals. Later returning partial anatomy lacks the current material/source proof required to preserve the logical finger. The prior quarantine-search extension improved some analytic tracking counts but increased logical replacements and changed no output on the actual recorded corpus. That rejected shortcut is not revived here.

## E. Calibration proof and retained uncertainty

Two new targeted controller experiments distinguish perception failure from sample-collection behavior.

First, qualified analytic input runs through the actual Laboratory controller, readiness and sample windows at 15, 10 and 5 Hz. All three relative five-training-plus-five-held-out journeys and all three absolute nine-plus-five journeys reach their playground. Relative input uses the actual image tracker, gradual pixel motion, a genuinely proved model-source alias at target three and registered index-only input at target four, retaining one logical index. Synthetic raw relative P95 is 0.01633/0.01767/0.01767 of plane diagonal; absolute is approximately 0.0000482. These values show independent validator behavior on controlled inputs, not physical accuracy. Absolute cadence cases use a stable raw hand source; they do not establish absolute continuity across neural source aliases.

Second, the exact chronological 1,336 recorded producer outputs are fed through the real controller. Target one saves at source time 6.011 seconds. Target two encounters motion and then forward/backward rejection, with some qualified tracking returning at 7.728. The producer later expires the original index and creates a new logical index at 16.145. At 16.462 the controller suspends the saved target evidence rather than transferring it to the replacement. The remaining 1,039 frames produce zero sample pushes while suspended. No fit or held-out sequence completes. This recording follows diagnostic prompts, not the current calibration target; the experiment proves the lineage-hold mechanism and does not reconstruct the owner's actual later calibration attempt or label ordinary movement as an erroneous rejection.

The current 150 ms display freshness watchdog does not erase a valid sample window solely because inference is 5 Hz. Fresh measurement admission, observed bad frames and required fifteen samples plus 600 ms qualified evidence still apply. Current low-rate success does not justify accepting stale coordinates.

The unresolved automatic recovery requirement is upstream: re-establish correct selected-index identity and a current measured endpoint after substantial pose change, without accepting a plausible wrong neural digit or a persistent background patch. Retaining completed targets is not recovery. Geometry-only identity transfer, indefinite timer renewal, mask presence and manual owner confirmation are not substituted for that missing proof.

An independent controller/source review challenges wrong-digit relabelling, face/background reference contamination, hidden cross-reference rivals, source-alias laundering, contrary gesture followed by model absence, shaft/template origin confusion, stale completion and controller transfer to a new ready track. No new production path is adopted, so those threats are not declared solved by unexercised research code. Existing regression assertions and the original failing motion test remain intact.

## G. Physical and performance limits

No sleeping-owner webcam trial, full physical calibration, independent physical accuracy, true motion lag or worst-case device guarantee is fabricated. Sparse development annotations cannot establish zero false associations across all unsampled frames. A visible finger may still be missed, mislabelled by the neural hand model, or remain ineligible after appearance changes. The current preview is useful for another test but is not described as rock-solid.

A fresh handoff check also preserves a lifecycle limitation: the old idle tab initially played 640×480 video but admitted zero inference observations and displayed stale tracking, without a console error. Its cause was not established. Stopping the camera and reloading that idle tab restored current GPU processing: 324 accepted observations at the read, recent 14.2 Hz under the 15 Hz normal ceiling, capture-to-inference P95 8.9 ms and capture-to-canvas-submission P95 23.1 ms. These are rendered session diagnostics, not sensor-to-display latency or a human pointing trial. The first failure is retained, rather than calling the whole startup sequence unconditionally successful. The camera is stopped again for handoff.

Administrator-governed default ceilings remain normal foreground 15 Hz, active calibration 60 Hz, model 60 Hz and visible unfocused 8 Hz; hidden capture stops. The rate policy remains editable in Admiralty, whereas the 150 ms safety bound is immutable. One-in-flight inference, no backlog, distinct presented-frame identity, pressure backoff and RGB/CPU baseline remain unchanged. Optional hardware is not required. The new rejected providers add no runtime cost, model download, camera request, telemetry field or automatic hardware switch to the product.
