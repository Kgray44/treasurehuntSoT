## Source and tracking boundaries

Observed aspect-correct 3D index geometry supplies index anatomy and end-on posture features. The index chain, not a wrist-dependent palm center, supplies model association. Cropped or inferred other fingers cannot veto valid observed index evidence merely through their absence. Coherent projected conflicting fingers, a folded index, pinch or degenerate geometry still abstain. Index-only intent remains a heuristic; neither missing fingers nor monocular z proves intent.

Absolute pointing uses `INDEX_TIP_AXIS_V1` features with `INDEX_CHAIN_V1` association and `INDEX_TIP_AXIS_RIDGE_V1` mapping. Relative movement uses only fresh image-tip position `[1,x,y,0,0]`, `INDEX_TIP_IMAGE_V1`, `INDEX_IMAGE_TRACK_V1` and `INDEX_TIP_DISPLACEMENT_V2`. Collection, fit, independent check and estimation reject old or mismatched profiles, nonzero image axes and incompatible source bindings. Fits are not silently upgraded.

The local image tracker uses bounded appearance correlation, texture, uniqueness and forward/backward agreement. Its algorithm identity is distinct from the pinned hand model digest. It exposes no invented hand skeleton, 3D coordinates or probability. Same-capture model results can initialize the tip. Coherent full-hand evidence takes priority for identifying the index and authorizing pointing; full-hand open/bent posture, ambiguity or an index/image conflict pauses collection and cannot be bypassed by an image hint. The finger-only fallback applies when full-hand evidence is unavailable. The image point remains the relative coordinate source throughout, so model return cannot snap or silently reanchor the cursor. A confirmed contrary full-hand posture requires compatible pointing evidence again, or an explicit new hint once the whole hand is unavailable. A manual visible-tip hint provides a cold-start fallback when the hand model cannot initialize. It is human selection, not an automatic finger classifier. Every manual reseed creates a new image identity. Fresh image observations have independent strict metadata, ordering, frame, lease and 150 ms freshness gates. Image buffers are transient and released on stop or hiding.

The camera remains broker-owned, with one inference in flight and no stale queue. The adaptive scheduler targets available new video frames, slows under measured processing or event-loop pressure, and reduces work when the visible page loses focus. Hidden pages stop capture. Software timing excludes camera exposure and display presentation. A fixed template/motion search has finite speed, texture, blur and edge limits; failure must pause rather than claim fresh tracking.

## Calibration and user feedback

Relative image tracking uses one producer from training through independent check and playground, so a full-hand return does not swap feature definitions or hand IDs into a fit. Bad frames immediately pause collection. Buffered readiness allows natural recovery without starving good interspersed frames. Completed targets survive temporary loss; an actual new source, selected fingertip or view change leaves a persistent focused explanation requiring fresh setup. The sample window retains its 15 samples, 600 ms observed stable evidence, bounded recovery and independent raw five-target P95 limit of 3% of the plane diagonal. Display smoothing and last-seen markers cannot enter samples or refresh evidence timestamps.

The display and evidence clocks have separate jobs. Results older than 150 ms cannot supply a visible actionable point or new sample. A display-age watchdog hides stale output but does not treat the absence of a newly delivered result as a newly observed bad capture. Calibration continuity instead uses accepted capture timestamps: adjacent captures must be no more than 300 ms apart, with at most 100 ms credited per continuous pair. An actual rejected model/image result, ineligible capture or explicit user pause breaks the evidence segment; longer capture gaps split it automatically. Thus a valid delayed stream can retain agreeing evidence without crediting real rejected or paused intervals. Source freshness, bindings, minimum samples, stable spread and independent accuracy remain unchanged.

## Gaming-load throughput and supported appearance recovery

The current source uses exact integral-moment NCC with the original complete candidate domain, tie order, correlation, texture and uniqueness requirements. The runtime posts the same captured bitmap to the model before computing grayscale/image candidates. Prepared image work is not an observation; same-capture validated model authority and a final 150 ms clock check must commit it. Stale, replaced, invalid or discarded transactions cannot update identity/reference clocks or produce calibration evidence.

The pacer removes the extra 25% idle allowance applied to elapsed worker time, which already included scheduler delays under gaming load. One inference remains in flight, with no queue. Main-thread work remains bounded to 30%. Normal focused tracking defaults to 15 Hz; active unpaused TRAINING and VALIDATION use a 60 Hz phase ceiling. Paused sessions, PREPARE, RESULTS and PLAYGROUND use the normal ceiling. Visible unfocused capture remains capped at 8 Hz, and hidden pages stop capture. A separate model ceiling defaults to 60 Hz; the lower applicable phase/model cap limits effective scheduling. Pressure may reduce rates further, and switching to the higher calibration ceiling retains bounded recovery. Fresh-video callbacks wake a waiting capture without another full polling interval. These are ceilings and budgets, not guaranteed physical FPS or whole-system utilization. Historical receipts retain their named source's policy, including earlier normal 30 Hz and focused 60 Hz ceilings.

Appearance reference renewal is tightly scoped: only a failed reference and full-quality adaptive optical match can qualify; the same full-hand pointing source must agree within two analysis pixels for three fresh frames spanning 120 ms, with gaps at most 250 ms. Stable reference matches never renew; contrary/ambiguous/conflicting/expired evidence cannot renew. Renewal preserves optical position and identity. Unsupported finger-only appearance remains bounded to 1,200 ms.
