## Architecture and result

The private candidate retains a nonactionable search origin under the existing `IndexImageTracker` owner while immutable selected material moves beyond the frozen accepted window. Unlike the earlier rejected prototype, it conditionally lets current material support start an unassociated anatomical cohort. It requires the exact previously trusted source, current full pointing geometry and four named index joints, old DIP plus a separated proximal image match, and stable-tip agreement. Foreign-source proof keeps the original search domain. A new association starts the existing three-frame/120 ms probation; it does not inherit an already mature unassociated cohort.

That probation measures geometry continuity, not three independent terminal observations. Source review also finds that pointing probation can advance from raw moving-tip agreement before the body proof passes. Neither behavior is adopted into the application.

The paired positive passes: the candidate produces a current analytic measurement with the original logical identity after three returning observations spanning 133 ms, while its matched canonical baseline remains unassociated without a point. Seven other controls pass, covering held model dropout, copied appearance with no model, foreign-source exclusion, frozen-window rivals, gap/context and stationary compatibility, stale rollback, wrong geometry and blank input. These generated pixels and software labels are not physical fingertip truth.

The corrected adversarial control preserves original DIP/proximal texture, removes the original terminal patch and places its texture at a different plausible endpoint. Current neural TIP8 agrees with that transplanted patch. After three observations spanning 133 ms, the candidate returns `TRACKING` and `FULL_HAND_POINT_CONFIRMED` with a non-null accepted point while old-part support is true. This violates the required abstention. The transplanted patch overlaps the former terminal footprint; this is not a detached-silhouette test or a measured anatomical finger switch. No paired baseline false-position rate is claimed.

## Structural recovery and re-registration

The already completed root-connected screen remains negative: zero proposals on 77 later RIGHT frames, with 75 palm-transport refusals before segmentation and two component refusals. It is not repeated or retuned. A proposed temporal boundary tracker would additionally need independently visible root/web ownership. A smooth shaft and cap can instead follow a middle finger when the index retracts behind the palm; hidden attachments need not create an observable topology change. The retained mask alone supplies no such ownership certificate.

A proposed stricter body-derived similarity check could reject the displaced transplant above, but a copy at its predicted tip location has zero geometric residual while the real distal finger bends or becomes occluded. It therefore still lacks independent terminal evidence. That variation is stopped before implementation. Three-frame repetition cannot turn the same unsupported endpoint into proof. No new reference, source alias or production recovery authority is adopted.

A narrow readback of the original mixed-motion tape further rejects that variation's prerequisite: at the last measured observation before the first loss, stored PIP and DIP anchors exist, but are less than 15 analysis pixels apart and belong to an older image than the accepted optical state. There are zero qualifying separated DIP/proximal pairs and no co-captured body/tip reference for that proposed fit. This reads existing scalar graph data only; no pixel blob or tracker replay is executed. It is not evidence that the tape contains no DIP anchor.
