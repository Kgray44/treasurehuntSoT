## Intended experience

The existing Device connections entry, large camera stage, target plane, conventional controls and relative/absolute workflows remain the same. Camera connection and detected joints are distinct from recognized posture and collection eligibility. The current target freezes during uncertainty, explains recovery, and resumes naturally; completed targets remain visible. A genuine source, hand-generation or view change explicitly requires fresh setup.

Bad or absent frames stop collection and fresh pointer estimates immediately. In the successor, an established same-track hand can recover on the next good frame after an isolated posture/framing dip shorter than 180 ms. Actual tracking loss, ambiguity or sustained uncertainty needs 120 ms of fresh eligible anatomy. Initial or replacement selection settles for 250 ms. Existing provider spatial matching has a 1.2-second absence grace; ambiguous crossings and unproven jumps still create new generations. This is local continuity inference, never biometric identity.

The unfinished hold retains agreeing evidence for up to 2.5 seconds, within a five-second wall-time window. Paused and rejected intervals contribute no evidence time. Fifteen samples and at least 600 ms of observed stable evidence remain required. A sustained coherent replacement hold can replace only the unfinished target. There is no forced 20-second retry. Explicit pause, resume and retry remain available.

## Geometry and integrity

Posture uses aspect-corrected inferred x/y/z joint relationships, palm coherence, index bend and separation from competing fingers. Relaxed fingers and a gently bent index are eligible when the index is distinguishable. Open or softly open palms, competing fingers, pinches, folded/hooked indices and collapsed joints abstain. Camera-facing index foreshortening no longer relies on a short projected distal segment alone.

Absolute features use the full index chord with depth-aware palm normalization and the versioned `INDEX_AXIS_RIDGE_V2` mapping. These are empirical camera-relative features. The existing worker returns normalized image landmarks with inferred relative z, not world coordinates or metric depth. [MediaPipe's coordinate documentation](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/master/docs/solutions/hands.md) describes this distinction. Occlusion and uncertain monocular inference can still prevent eligibility or a successful accuracy check.

The pinned model, worker, WASM, camera capture and rendering implementation are preserved. The local observation adapter advances to version 1.2.0: palm association is independent of fingertip eligibility, and partial or temporarily absent palms retain their old records without refreshing timestamps. Observation freshness, ordered timestamps, source/camera/view bindings, held-out targets, raw error and jitter thresholds, supported bounds and privacy/export restrictions remain enforced. Old samples are never rebound to a new hand generation. Long loss can therefore require fresh setup when local identity has expired, even though a shorter posture interruption preserves completed targets.

## Wrist clipping, motion and check-result follow-up

The owner reported bottom-target framing rejection, intermittent motion loss, superimposed duplicate hand hypotheses and an unexplained request to repeat five completed checks. The successor keeps the wrist optional. It requires the observed index chain and knuckle span, then uses an observed knuckle arch or nondegenerate finger relationships when wrist orientation is unavailable. The latter does not claim a palm normal. A clipped non-index fingertip contributes only when its remaining observed joints show a clear bend; cropping an open palm cannot manufacture pointing intent. Exact camera-facing index overlap is tested with an unchanged palm and inferred relative depth. Missing index/core joints and ambiguous competing fingers still abstain.

Recent local palm motion may bridge a small matching gap only with two reliable prior observations, at most 200 ms separation, bounded velocity and prediction residual, and coherent measured palm scale (maximum/minimum ratio at most 1.2). Ordinary spatial matching and ambiguity retain precedence. Near-identical duplicate hypotheses require matching known handedness, visibility, relative depth, joints and bones; established two-track overlap is never deduplicated. Handedness scores remain handedness scores, not fabricated tracking confidence. A first abrupt unproven jump and visually indistinguishable physical overlap remain uncertain.

Display smoothing uses a velocity-filtered adaptive cutoff inspired by the authors' [1 Euro filter](https://gery.casiez.net/1euro/). Deliberate motion raises responsiveness while stationary noise receives stronger smoothing. A single unexpected jump is held for confirmation without contaminating velocity; coherent continuation can confirm on the next interval. Unconfirmed display holds expire after 150 ms from the last accepted estimate. After interruption a faded, dashed **Last seen** marker expires within 450 ms of that same accepted estimate. Rejected inputs never refresh its timestamp. It cannot enter calibration, held-out error, export or practice actions. Raw samples and the independent 3% diagonal P95 accuracy requirement remain unchanged.

Results now state that all five check targets were recorded, show measured raw P95 error and its limit, explain why retry is offered, and retain the fit. Steady-hold jitter and maximum error remain diagnostics with no added pass/fail rule. Rounded near-boundary values explicitly say when the unrounded error is just above the limit. A failed check never silently starts another check or unlocks the playground.
