# Session epoch binding amendment proposal

**UNAPPROVED.** This is a separately reviewable proposal accompanying the [session epoch investigation](Phase_1_Session_Epoch_Architecture_20261021.md). It changes no effective governing document, runtime binding, qualification gate or published interaction definition. The owner directive authorizes isolated research and this proposal; it does not accept the production policy below. Current authority remains the [effective Spatial authority](../../Governing/Voyagewright_Spatial_Experience_Effective_Authority.md) and its preserved sources and amendments.

The proposed extension concerns only a frozen **relative** mapping evaluated on fresh measurements from a new local tracking epoch. It does not establish original physical-finger continuity, permit absolute portability, resume an earlier operation or transfer old samples to a replacement.

## Proposed relative confirmation and binding policy

The following requirements are proposed text for owner review, not current authority.

1. A pointing session may preserve its place across local epoch changes. Each epoch must identify its actual source context and capture boundary. Local readiness grants no old calibration permission, physical identity, source alias, intent or actionable screen coordinate. Acquisition must retain the canonical current-anatomy, ambiguity, contrary-evidence, freshness and probation protections; resetting an epoch is not a substitute for satisfying them.
2. The old fit, training groups, completed targets, original binding, validation, supported region and expiry remain immutable evidence under their original provenance. They are not pooled with new groups or relabeled as observations of the new epoch. The existing calibration owner remains the sole evidence and mapping owner; the session coordinator holds references and transition tokens, not a second calibration ledger.
3. Compatibility checking is limited to the same user/device/provider and compatible camera, model, feature-family, mirror/orientation, geometry and display domain. A software track change alone does not prove incompatibility or compatibility. An incompatible or unproved domain requires separate setup; absolute mapping reuse is unsupported by this proposal.
4. Before the first check, freeze the old relative coefficients, supported feature bounds and original expiry. Do not estimate an offset, scale, rotation, new reference or coefficient from the checking targets. Use five distinct canonical held-out targets and wholly fresh new-epoch groups, each with at least 15 eligible samples spanning 600 ms. Preserve current source-pixel age at most 150 ms, sample ordering, interruption handling, raw error calculation and the raw P95 limit of 3% of the viewport diagonal. No old group or old validation pass contributes to this decision.
5. Collect checks only from current eligible measurements bound to the new epoch. During checking, the old validated cursor and action authority remain unavailable. Any provisional feedback must be explicitly distinguished from calibrated output. Closure, stale callbacks, identity uncertainty, domain changes and expiry must fail closed; restarting an attempt cannot credit elapsed interruption time or recycle prior check groups.
6. A complete passing check produces a compatibility proposal recording the original fit, its unchanged limits, the actual new binding and the fresh validation provenance. It is not a physical-association certificate or action token. The research prototype stops here. If a production adoption policy is later approved, only the existing calibration owner may issue a distinct current mapping record bound to that epoch and its fresh validation. The old record stays unchanged; original expiry and coverage cannot be extended. Exact binding checks remain effective for every consumer.
7. A failed, incomplete or unsupported check leaves the old evidence suspended. Offer a separately bound fresh setup and conventional controls without a recurring automatic target-one reset loop. New training and validation remain separate. Neither preserving the old record nor displaying a new local preview is a successful calibration recovery.

## Unchanged protections and required evidence

Permission, camera-lease ownership, provider generations, lifecycle shutdown, private evidence handling and late-result rejection remain unchanged. Relative and absolute qualification remain separate. No fabricated depth, confidence, predicted/held calibration sample, old identity inheritance, template self-confirmation or loss-induced activation is permitted. Current 15-sample/600 ms collection and five-check/raw-3% gates cannot be relaxed to obtain a compatibility pass. Session changes cannot revive an ended interaction or make the rejected transplanted-terminal sequence actionable through an old fit.

Before an adoption decision, focused evidence must cover equivalent relative coordinates; biased, scaled, rotated and out-of-coverage replacements; exact error and expiry boundaries; stale/reordered/closed-epoch callbacks; camera/model/mirror/viewport changes; manual-selection protection; interrupted/reused/incomplete check groups; and rejected absolute reuse. Retain wrong-digit, alternate-hand, copied-terminal and model-dropout cases. Distinguish a false local acquisition from any subsequent calibration authority; a successful screen check alone does not prove anatomical identity. Retain original failures and test the existing authority boundaries rather than deleting their assertions.

Measure four outcomes separately: current local acquisition, original physical continuity, relative mapping compatibility and useful session progress. A meaningful continuation must complete independent compatibility checking or a new valid setup without manual track repair; report time to usable mapping, repeated or discarded targets, failed/abstaining trials and unsafe transitions. Local-ID counts and preserved-but-unusable targets are insufficient. Synthetic and recorded evidence can test contracts and mathematics; physical webcam accuracy, usability and final qualification remain open under the [qualification plan](Project_Reach_Requirements_and_Qualification_Plan.md).

## Current research disposition

The first isolated run retains **40 passing and two failing tests out of 42**. All 18 numerical compatibility tests and 20 session-coordinator tests pass. Both copied-terminal perception negatives fail. The unchanged first run is preserved; a public-source reproduction adds bounded diagnostics without changing the algorithm or assertions. It observes four points and zero readiness-eligible observations across nine uninterrupted returning captures; all four points retain the original software ID. The cold-reset arm emits seven points and two readiness-eligible observations across nine returning captures. The first uninterrupted run's later readiness assertion was not reached; the successor diagnostic separately observes zero of nine ready. The separate exact-binding test still refuses the old calibration on replacement bindings. These generated controls are not physical identity labels.

Consequently, canonical local `TRACKING` or `READY` is insufficient as the sole independent current-index validity gate for this architecture. The false-position limitation is not caused solely by reset. Fresh five-target checking can measure numerical mapping compatibility; it cannot certify anatomical index identity or make a copied terminal valid. Preserved assertions and failed results remain part of the research artifact rather than being weakened or counted as production coverage.

The frozen-fit policy cannot reuse partial target-three training: there is no completed old fit to check, and no transfer of those completed target groups is demonstrated.

**The current prototype is not eligible for application integration or approval as an implementation of this proposal.** No owner approval of the unsafe candidate is requested. A future adoption proposal must close the applicable failed safety negatives and establish adequate current local-measurement proof before any mapping permission is considered. Owner review cannot waive the accuracy, identity, freshness or qualification protections, and cannot convert the present mathematical partial result into safe recovery.

## Owner decision still required

The reviewer may reject this policy, keep it research-only or request an explicit revised amendment. Approval must state whether five fresh relative checks are sufficient for the narrow calibration confirmation boundary, which exact binding fields may differ, the adopted version and consumer behavior, and the source-bound safety and experience evidence supporting that decision. It must not imply original physical identity, absolute portability or resumption of an earlier operation.

Any accepted effective change requires the governing and Drydock compatibility review, relevant feature and user guidance review, focused validation and ordinary Sounding Line/protected integration requirements. Neither this proposal, a local test pass nor a new mapping record supplies release authority. No production adoption or owner acceptance is recorded here.
