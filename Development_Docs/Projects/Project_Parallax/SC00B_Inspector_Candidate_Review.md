## Shared-branch integration seams

- The selected-object Inspector currently derives `currentEntity` from `selected` and sends a missing entity to Scene settings. SC-00's pending typed `selectionItems`, room-feature selection, and group selection must be preserved when integrating this branch. Those selections need their own contextual identity, supported actions and canonical editors; a non-entity selection must not be treated as an empty scene.
- SC-01's versioned Environment and independent Scene interfaces belong behind the scene-level path and its canonical save/history boundaries. Avoid adding parallel Environment or Scene mutations to the generic `SpatialEntity` controls. SC-02's emerging elements likewise need their typed editors rather than generic fields with no effective mutation.
- `SpatialStudio.module.css` has a responsive Inspector overlay at 1099 px and a full-grid containing block at 700 px. SC-00C's Library and SC-02's viewport/control layouts also touch these breakpoints. Preserve their separate dock and canvas constraints during the combined merge and rerun a 390 px actual-browser journey, including the three rail destinations and keyboard traversal.
