## First owner test and selection gate

1. Establish entitlement and a trusted, appropriately licensed origin. First run a minimal authored note on a clear desk through the real Lens. If the SDK cannot initialize on Safari, retain the error and stop that provider's qualification; compare the next authorized engine instead of spending days rewriting tracking.
2. Record exact source commit, provider/SDK version, iPhone model, iOS/Safari versions, test duration, lighting category and permission outcome. Keep photographs/maps local unless separately authorized. Align the same three ordered corners from two views, retaining a failed attempt as a failed attempt.
3. Walk sideways and around the desk for 60 seconds, then sustain the scene for five minutes. Observe translation/parallax against physical reference marks; aim for no visible jumps and no more than 5 cm displacement at the note. These are proposed owner-test thresholds, not attained measurements.
4. Inspect the actual note, confirm its canonical receipt, close/reopen Lens, and verify the same Voyage/publication/anchor identity. Verify animation and reduced-motion behavior. Revisit historical content without advancing progression.
5. Deliberately cover the camera, point at a low-feature area, briefly background Safari and return. Interactions must pause during uncertain tracking; recovery must preserve a verified map or explicitly require fresh registration. Record resets, recovery duration and whether alignment was repeated.
6. Export the local summary and separately record frame smoothness, Safari thermal behavior and observed drift. Assess sustained frame intervals against a 30 fps target and p95 interval below 50 ms; measure on the phone rather than importing host timings. Compare equivalent scenes/motions on each licensed candidate. An unavailable candidate is BLOCKED, never a numerical pass or score.
7. Select only after technical functioning and license fit are both established. Full reconstructed-room integration, occlusion and owner acceptance follow the first anchored-note result. Target completion is October 20 where feasible, October 22 at the seven-day objective; external gates may prevent that schedule.
