## Architecture and schema

One canonical Landfall domain extends existing qualification, confidence, geometry, providers and the shared foreground Chart controller. One Voyage's normal transaction remains the sole progression writer. Ephemeral guidance cannot directly mutate a Chronicle. Immutable editions pin authored context and media; the eight Captain commands, ordinary outdoor/virtual navigation and two-stage observations remain compatible.

There is **no database schema or migration change**. Optional JSON `context.regions` and `context.landmarks`, map levels, waypoint region/landmark IDs and route segment-region IDs extend existing definitions. Legacy definitions omit them. Validation enforces identity, Worldspace/reference, hierarchy, map/level, geometry, asset ownership, privacy and readable fallback.

## Regions, confidence and routes

Aligned floor/site overlays reuse existing geometry. Hierarchical site/building/floor/wing/room/gallery/corridor/exhibit/compact-outdoor/entrance/exit/stairs regions are flexible rather than museum-specific. Choosing a displayed floor does not confirm the Player's floor.

Typed position, heading, motion, elevation, regional observation and landmark evidence produces categorical `KNOWN`, `LIKELY`, `NEARBY`, `INFERRED`, `UNCERTAIN`, `UNAVAILABLE` or `CONFIRMED` state with reasons, scope, categories and expiry. Broad GPS can establish a site/building; fine indoor or exact-object GPS is capped at likely inside. Overlapping floors remain ambiguous. Hysteresis, freshness, bounded stable-context grace, plausible travel and impossible-jump rejection prevent noisy or indefinitely precise claims.

Corridor width, uncertainty, nearest segments, direction and continuity govern route matching. Useful movement continuity may narrow segment regions. Parallel paths, crossings, branches and indistinguishable floor paths retain ambiguity; backtracking and loops retain honest direction. Stationary/device-rotation hints cannot establish exact position.

## Creator and Player behavior

Creator controls upload and align protected overlays, name levels, draw hierarchical regions through existing tools or keyboard coordinates, associate waypoint/route regions, and configure positive/negative landmark references, guidance, frame requirements, privacy and canonical fallback. Publication blocks invalid private/public layouts, unavailable assets, missing mandatory fallback and exact targets without an effective independent verifier.

Field walks acquire optional foreground hints with separate consent and save definition/source-bound sanitized receipts. Edits mark them stale. Receipt availability labels do not expose raw fixes, headings, heights or imagery. Player embedded Chart and drawer share one controller, released geometry, readable context and floor controls. Captain sees sanitized canonical summaries and authorized fallback controls. Replay uses recorded categories and attribution without acquiring location, camera, heading or motion.

## Vision and exact-target authority

Explicit bounded camera comparison uses the existing `visionLocation` outcome vocabulary and a region-gated `VISION_WAYPOINT` source. Two to five distinct clear decoded views must agree with authored positive references; negatives, copies, malformed/low-detail or contradictory frames cannot confirm. This is conservative authored-view appearance comparison, not general object recognition or camera attestation. Different angles, lighting, crowds and occlusion can require fallback.

Before reading immutable protected references, the server requalifies plausible regional/ancestor geometry, availability, membership, CSRF/rate limits, edition/sequence, still count/bytes, raster format and decoded pixel bounds. Frames remain call-local. A process-local HMAC receipt expires within 30 seconds and binds actor, Voyage, edition, sequence, Worldspace, waypoint, landmark, region and definition hash. Restart requires a fresh comparison; client-declared success is rejected.

Exact-target `locationObservation` records matching qualified broad arrival to unlock the withheld prompt. A separate configured text answer, deliberate observation confirmation or Captain response completes through normal One Voyage verification. GPS arrival alone cannot verify an exact object. Mandatory visual/exact paths retain readable Player, Captain or alternate-waypoint fallback and canonical attribution.

## Sensors, privacy, offline and accessibility

Optional browser motion/orientation use explicit consent, throttled owned listeners and lifecycle cleanup. Qualified altitude uncertainty can indicate vertical transition; no absolute-height calibration is authored, so it cannot invent a floor label. Denial or unsupported capabilities retain Chart and fallback access. Background, sign-out, source/edition/history changes and unmount release acquisition.

Canonical history and field receipts contain IDs/categories/outcomes rather than live coordinates, sensor streams or Player frames. Context streams and signed landmark receipts cannot enter the offline outbox. Optional course/speed/elevation fields are stripped from accepted short-lived position delivery before encryption without extending expiry.

Phase 2's encrypted same-tab chart/pages/assets and expiring evidence requalification remain intact. Floor imagery shares the six-image/four-MiB bound; partial caching and online-only external tiles remain visible. Camera verification requires reconnect or fallback. Authorization, edition, sequence, TTL and logout clearing remain enforced.

Production acceptance covers compact/common phones, tablet and desktop, 200% text, keyboard/focus and pointer hit targets, nonvisual map alternatives, readable fallback, reduced motion, forced colors and scoped axe scans. Keyboard focus brings the complete objective action into view inside the smallest phone's scrollable tray.
