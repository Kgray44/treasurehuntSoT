## Frozen policy

Ordinary mainline MSES is fresh sentinel obligations + semantically affected suites/contracts + base-movement invalidations + dependency-selected cross-project evidence + missing required evidence + runtime/workspace conformance + exact candidate/train/tree binding evidence. Unknown mapping or affected debt requires a recorded conservative fallback and debt owner.

The sentinel spine is deliberately a small machine-readable _rule_, not guessed suite IDs: Sounding Line conformance is fresh for executable/authority runtime changes; static/type/policy follows source/gate policy; access/navigation is fresh for relevant global user-facing executable change; production build is fresh for executable/build change and may be omitted for record-only work. Prompt 3 owns the registry.
