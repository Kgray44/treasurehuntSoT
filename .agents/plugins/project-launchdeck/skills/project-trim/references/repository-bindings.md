## Minimum-sufficient context

Bind each important pointer to a repository commit/blob or known source revision when available. Confidence: EXACT, BOUNDED, COARSE, UNKNOWN. Staleness: FRESH, PARTIALLY_STALE, STALE, CONFLICTED, UNKNOWN. Never convert an unknown path or source conflict into assumed trust. Reuse fresh source-bound slices; refresh affected slices when main, scope, or authority changes. Accepted capsules compress prior proof but cannot replace present authority or proof for a new candidate. Reuse reads/searches by source identity, invalidate changed bindings, and record only safe metadata.

Focused repair/documentation normally starts with 3–6 pointers and one authority slice; bounded implementation/integration normally starts with 6–12 pointers and up to two authority slices. These are soft startup heuristics. READY status must be evidenced, not inferred from a familiar project name. Never refuse necessary source, history, test, or governing reads just to stay within a pointer count.

## Accounting and authority

Historical advisory target/warning/review bands: narrow 200k/300k/450k; ordinary 450k/700k/1M; high-risk 750k/1.1M/1.6M tokens. Large closure needs a defensible comparable baseline or is UNAVAILABLE. Bands warn about efficiency; they are not per-prompt limits or Goal budgets. Current authority may revise these snapshots.

The historical activity regimes (21.5k, 9k, 6k tokens/minute) are calibrated engineering estimates with wide ranges, not universal rates or billing. Launchdeck does not infer total usage from its own prompt bytes. Missing usage is null/UNAVAILABLE. It reports bytes, words, and a labeled UTF-8-bytes/4 coarse proxy only. No percentages of end-to-end savings are promised.

Sounding Line remains the repository's release authority where current governance requires it. Skills, packets, journal receipts, budget reports, telemetry, and capsules never emit RELEASE_GO. Explicit owner gates remain intact. Optional Bridgewatch and model routing were evaluated but not adopted in the historical record; this plugin does not enable them.
