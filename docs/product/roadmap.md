# Roadmap

## Available

Core Chronicle authoring, Voyage hosting, player progression surfaces, profile identity, protected content foundations, and presentation systems.

## In development

Community Harbor convergence, broader validation of external operational integrations, and refinement of connected role experiences.

## Planned and research

Future Community Harbor, Landfall, and Vision Waypoints work remains subject to accepted design and validation. Dates are not committed here.

## Out of scope

This repository does not promise a generic game engine, unrestricted public hosting, or automatic publication of private Chronicle material.
