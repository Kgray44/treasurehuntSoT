# Voyagewright overview

Voyagewright is a private home for authored story experiences shared with the people invited to them. A **Chronicle** is the authored story material; a **Voyage** is a prepared experience of a particular published Chronicle edition.

Players join Voyages and revisit their history. Captains prepare and guide shared Voyages. Creators use Creator Studio to arrange story material, locations, media, artifacts, and editions. Voyagewright connects those experiences while remaining focused on stories rather than becoming a generic game engine or map service.

Published editions stay unchanged once released, so a Voyage can continue with the version it began with. Privacy and consent are central: people see only material shared with them. See [features](features.md) and [terminology](terminology.md).
