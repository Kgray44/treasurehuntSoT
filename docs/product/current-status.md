# Current status

**Available on main:** identity, role-aware Player/Captain/Creator flows, Studio editing and publishing, invitations, libraries, journals, profile and Passport surfaces, protected content foundations, animation presentation, and compatibility routes.

**Development-only or compatibility-only:** development showcases, legacy routes, and package-exchange integrations are not a promise of a generally hosted service.

**Externally unvalidated:** live third-party storage, scanning, and provider behavior require an appropriately configured environment; local adapters and tests do not establish live-provider proof.

**Planned:** broader Community Harbor operations and future experience work are tracked in the [roadmap](roadmap.md). Historic program records are evidence, not a release promise.
