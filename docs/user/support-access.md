# Support Access

Support Access is a temporary, consent-based way to let authorized support staff see limited account information while helping you. It never gives them your password or control of your account.

Before you approve a request, Voyagewright shows its purpose and the information it can access. You can deny, cancel, or revoke access at any time. If a request does not match the help you asked for, deny it and contact the service owner through a trusted channel.

Some support requests can offer a named repair. You see the proposed action before deciding whether to approve it, and you can approve diagnosis without approving a repair.
