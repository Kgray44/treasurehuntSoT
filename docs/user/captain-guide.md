# Captain guide

Use the Captain Library to choose a Chronicle, prepare a Voyage, and invite your crew. Invitees see the Voyage in their own library when they have access. If someone cannot join, check the invitation and their account before sharing any private Chronicle material.

## Choose your participation

When you create a Voyage, choose **Captain only** if you are running it without joining the crew, or **Captain + Player** if you will experience it with them. Captain + Player gives you the normal Player view alongside separate Captain controls; it does not create another account or grant broader Player access.

## Prepare and begin

Use **Open Muster Room** to review the Chronicle, crew invitations, readiness, and connection state before launch. In a Captain + Player Voyage, use the Player waiting room to begin once the crew is ready. **Leave Waiting Room** returns to your library; **Leave Voyage**, Captaincy changes, and cancellation are separate choices.

## Guide the Voyage

The Captain's Console shows the current Voyage and the actions available to you. Review an action's effect before confirming it. Captain controls do not reveal Player-private information, Creator notes, drafts, hidden answers, or private account details.

## Change Captains safely

Use **Transfer Captaincy** to choose another joined Player as Captain. **Relinquish Captaincy** puts a shared Voyage into **Succession Hold** until someone takes over; it does not cancel the Voyage. **Cancel Voyage for Everyone** is a separate action.
