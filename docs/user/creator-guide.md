# Creator guide

Creator Studio is where you build a Chronicle. Create or choose a Chronicle, then use its settings, story blocks, locations, media, artifacts, and editions to shape the experience. Published editions stay unchanged so active Voyages can rely on them.

## Build your story

Select a Story Block to open its Inspector. **Guided** mode focuses on the next authoring step, **Detailed** shows the available controls, and **Engineering** offers advanced information for experienced Creators. Choose destinations by their chapter and block title, then use the visual editor to build the available conditions.

You can save a Passage, fragment, or Chapter as a private reusable item. Before inserting it into a draft, Studio shows a preview. Undo and Redo treat the insertion as one normal edit.

## Review and publish

Open **Versions**, then choose **Review and publish**. Save your draft, review the available readiness guidance, changes, media, and release notes, then confirm publication when everything is ready. A successful publication creates an immutable edition and offers the next available actions, such as previewing it or preparing a Voyage.

If publication cannot continue, your draft stays available for review and repair. Use only material you are authorized to handle, especially when importing or sharing private content.
