# Chronicle Passport

Chronicle Passport is your private personal archive. It brings together the Voyages you joined, Timeline, People, statistics, memories, keepsakes, artifacts, saved Community material, and the exact Chronicle editions that matter to your history. It is not Personal Harbor and it is not your public Profile.

Choose **Open Voyage** to revisit a Journey Summary, path, crew, artifacts, edition details, and remembrance. **Add Memory** lets you keep a private reflection. Some details may be unavailable if they were never retained; Voyagewright shows that honestly rather than inventing a record.

Timeline organizes your history by year. People helps you revisit shared Voyages. Statistics are private and noncompetitive. Your Artifact Cabinet records items associated with you, while a shared artifact moment records what a crew witnessed.

Use [Profile](profile.md) to manage your public identity, and [Personal Harbor](personal-harbor.md) for account settings.
