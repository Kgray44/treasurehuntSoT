# Player guide

The NCL-01 native 3D candidate is still in validation. A supported candidate
companion can load a released chest or compass, place it on a detected surface
and inspect the actual object; unsupported scenes retain Guided View.

Build **0.1.0 (7)** is available in internal TestFlight with the approved official
dark brass compass icon, made only by cropping/resizing the original artwork.
The app uses the stable address
[ncl01.absoluterelativesystems.com](https://ncl01.absoluterelativesystems.com).
Choose **Update → Open** and use the same account. No new invitation or certificate
profile is required. Changing Wi-Fi no longer itself requires another app build;
the laptop must stay awake and online with its preview services running. The
[deployment instructions](../../Development_Docs/Projects/Project_Parallax/NCL01_TestFlight_Deployment.md)
include saved start/stop procedures.

Build 7 changes the artwork only. The account, stable address and AR controls
are the same. The separate October 21 completion candidate is in qualification
and has not replaced that installed beta.

In that candidate, the native Lens explains model preparation, surface search
and limited tracking. Tap a visible object, or aim at it and choose **Select
object at center**, then choose **Inspect selected
object** to open the result in the Player. During an active passage, the Player
records an authorized observation before showing accepted inspection. Revisiting
a passage shows its inspection without recording a new observation or changing
progress. A connection or access failure stays visible and does not advance the
Voyage. **Return to Guided View** remains
available while the camera is preparing or waiting. These changes still require
physical acceptance and protected integration; current software results are in the
[completion ledger](../../Development_Docs/Projects/Project_Parallax/NCL01_Full_Completion_20261021.md).

Find **The Classroom Compass Trail — NCL01** under **My Chronicle Library → Active
Voyages**. The owner reports objects appearing when looking around in an earlier
native camera trial, following an initially empty view. The current layout uses
manual floor alignment; its authored appearance/placement has not been accepted.
Automatic room registration belongs to later spatial-fidelity work. NCL-01
preserves the authored relative layout at the selected local surface origin. Exact
build-7 installation/native behavior, all three objects and tracking/interaction
quality are unverified. The [native engineering record](../../Development_Docs/Projects/Project_Parallax/NCL01_Native_Scene_Vertical_Slice.md)
preserves the remaining acceptance gates.

In the active Journal, choose **Connect another device** and scan the code with
your phone, or choose **Use a code instead**. Sign in with the same Voyagewright
account on the phone and join the Chronicle. **Continue the Chronicle** closes
the connection dialog and returns to your place in the book.

On the companion, **Change companion purpose** offers **Chronicle Lens**,
**Voyage Chart** and **Journal companion** when their released content is
available. Lens opens the actual spatial clue in Guided View; Chart keeps the
current objective visible; Journal companion presents released passages with
standard or larger reading size. **More purposes** includes released artifacts
and the crew display. Unavailable purposes explain what is missing.
**Return to Chronicle** opens the full Journal for the same Voyage. The Journal
remembers your reading position; **Return to Current Objective** takes you to the
latest passage.

If an opening effect cannot finish, the Journal explains its readable fallback
above the pages. Once the pages are ready, **Continue Voyage** remains available
to advance the current Voyage.

The Journal's **Send to...** lets you choose another
active device and send the current released map, note or artifact. Choose **Receive**
on that device to open it. Your original view stays recoverable while it arrives.
Private notes require confirmation. If the controlling device goes away, choose
**Recover view here** on an available personal device. Crew displays show only
content explicitly shared with the crew. Connected screens refresh when a transfer
changes. Returning to the page or restoring your connection checks the current
view immediately; interrupted updates remain recoverable. Physical-device and
owner walkthrough acceptance remain separate from the integrated software.

Use Personal Harbor's device preferences and security controls to review or
remove account sessions. See [account security](account-security.md).

When a released passage includes a spatial moment, choose **Open Chronicle Lens** to read and interact with its clue in Guided View. The same clue stays readable without a camera, while seated or using a keyboard. A compatible native companion can offer local camera placement. Closing the camera, using Android Back, or losing its permission returns the moment to Guided View; reopen it explicitly for a fresh camera session. Lens interactions are recorded separately from Voyage progression. Revisited passages preserve their meaning without changing progress.

Where signed region preparation is enabled, an optional offline region panel appears in the Journal map.
Select **Prepare offline region** to check availability, size, included released
resources and authorization expiry; select **Download offline region** separately
to download and verify them. Partial downloads can be resumed, and stale regions
need a refresh. Removing a device's region preserves saved Chronicle history.
External tiles and online suggestions require a connection. Availability depends
on the deployment's signing configuration. Native use also requires an origin-bound release qualified for your device.

In a configured native companion, background reminders are optional. Enable them
only when you want a broad reminder for the released objective, with separate
background-location and notification permission. A generic notice opens a newly
authorized Player destination; it does not confirm a visit. Return to the current
Chart and choose **Use my location** for a fresh foreground check. **Disable
reminders** clears the device's registration and pending hints. Delivery and
reboot recovery remain subject to device behavior and signed-release qualification. Emulator/Simulator acceptance does not establish physical-device delivery or energy performance.

Optional nearby-device hints use a short exchange prepared on both devices. The
exchange expires even if the devices' clocks differ; prepare it again after a
pause or expiry. A peer hint does not confirm a visit. A notice return may require
sign-in again when your account session is absent or expired. If you are still
signed in but Voyage access was removed or its pinned session is unavailable,
the return goes to your Player home. A saved session never restores removed
Voyage access.

After accepting an invitation, use the Player Library to find the Voyages available to you. A Voyage can show a waiting state until your Captain begins it. During play, use the story view and Chronicle Journal to follow events, revisit earlier pages, and return to your history when available.

If the published Voyage includes Landfall, open **map** in the Journal tools to see the released Voyage Chart. On a physical chart, select **Use my location** when you want a live position and its estimated accuracy. Select **Stop using my location** to stop; closing the map, leaving the Journal, pausing the Voyage, or putting the tab in the background also stops it. If permission is denied or the signal is weak, the chart stays available without a reliable position marker. A virtual chart does not ask for browser location. A location signal is not a recorded visit until the Voyage confirms it.

If you are also the Captain, your Player view stays separate from your Captain controls. Your personal history and artifact eligibility follow your Player membership.

Indoor and compact Charts can show a likely region, uncertain floor or nearby target. Choosing a floor to view does not confirm that you are on it. Optional heading/motion hints need your explicit action and stop with foreground tracking. Reach a landmark's surrounding area before opening its camera, align with the described view, then ask to compare. A few transient stills are processed and are not saved. If comparison or permission is unavailable, use the configured observation or Captain path. Broad exact-target arrival unlocks its prompt; the separate response still completes the objective. Camera verification needs a connection, while released floor images share the bounded offline cache.

An active Living Chart Passage shows the same released map as the **map** drawer. Location remains off until you choose it. An observation Passage first requires an approved arrival, then unlocks its prompt and separate response. Arrival alone does not answer the observation.

When the Chronicle permits it, **Confirm arrival myself** sends the configured location-free fallback for server review. The chart distinguishes a local reading or queued evidence from a recorded visit. Once the offline shell and chart are prepared, the same browser tab can reload its released Landfall pages and cached first-party map images for up to 30 minutes. One encrypted evidence package survives reload for at most 90 seconds; reconnecting verifies access, edition, sequence, and evidence before recording a visit. Expired or changed-Voyage evidence needs a fresh reading. External map tiles require a connection. Sign-out or loss of authorized access clears the saved Voyage. Completed charts and arrival replay are presentation only and do not request location or change progress.

The Waiting Room shows safe crew invitation, readiness, and connection information. **Leave Waiting Room** returns to My Voyages. **Leave Voyage** ends only your membership after confirmation. If a Captain steps down, the Voyage can enter **Succession Hold** and show the choices available to you, such as taking Captaincy, continuing solo, or leaving.

See [Chronicle Passport](chronicle-passport.md) to revisit your private history.
