# Troubleshooting

## I cannot open a Voyage

Confirm that you used the intended account and invitation. Your Captain may need to renew the invitation or prepare the Voyage. Do not use another person's account or link.

## A page is stale or incomplete

Refresh the browser, then sign in again if prompted. If the problem continues, note the page and time without copying private content, then contact your Captain or support.

## I found a privacy or security concern

Stop sharing the material and use the private process in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
