# Troubleshooting

## I cannot open a Voyage

Confirm you used the intended account and invitation. A Captain may need to prepare the Voyage or renew the invitation. Do not use another person's link or account.

## A page is stale or incomplete

Refresh the browser, then sign in again if prompted. If the problem persists, record the route and time without copying private content, then contact the Captain or administrator.

## I found a privacy or security concern

Stop sharing the material and use the private process in [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
