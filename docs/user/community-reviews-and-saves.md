# Community reviews and saves




Community save counts, rating summaries, and reviews are derived from authoritative records. A review requires verified completion of the exact Chronicle release and a public Profile; Profile setup returns to the intended review composer. Eligible completed Chronicles also expose Review Chronicle from Passport history.




## Comments and spoiler details




Public listing conversations keep their preview-safe text visible. When a comment or Creator response has a spoiler detail, select its reveal control to request that detail; the spoiler is not part of the initial public view. Sign in with a Community Profile to comment, reply, or report a concern. Reports are sent to the moderated workflow rather than displayed in the conversation.
