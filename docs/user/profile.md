# Profile

Your Profile is the identity other people may see. In Personal Harbor, choose **Public Profile** to manage your display name, handle, biography, visibility, avatar, and banner.

Profile is not Chronicle Passport. Passport is your private Voyage history and archive; Profile contains only the information you choose to share. See [Chronicle Passport](chronicle-passport.md) and [privacy](privacy.md).
