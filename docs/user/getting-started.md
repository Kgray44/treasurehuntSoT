# Getting started

## Who this is for

Anyone joining a Chronicle as a Player, or preparing to create or run one.

1. Open the invitation or sign-in route supplied by your Captain.
2. Choose the role that matches your access: Player, Captain, or Creator.
3. Complete the available account or invitation step.
4. Open your library and select the Chronicle or Voyage you were invited to join.

You should arrive at the appropriate library or workspace. If an invitation fails, ask the Captain to verify it and see [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). Use only content you are authorized to view.

## Accessibility and privacy

Use your browser zoom and assistive technology preferences; see [accessibility](accessibility.md). Do not put private story material in public support requests; see [privacy](privacy.md).
