# Getting started

## Join Voyagewright

1. Open the invitation or sign-in link from your Captain.
2. Choose the available Player, Captain, or Creator experience that fits your access.
3. Complete the account or invitation step.
4. Open your library and choose the Chronicle or Voyage you were invited to join.

You should arrive at the right library or workspace. If an invitation does not work, ask your Captain to check it and see [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Accessibility and privacy

Use browser zoom and your assistive-technology preferences as needed; see [accessibility](accessibility.md). Keep private story material out of public support requests; see [privacy](privacy.md).
