# Private-content operations

Protected content must remain within the configured authorization, storage, scanning, derivative, and recovery boundaries. Use the repository's dedicated private-content commands only in an authorized environment with a named operational owner.

Preflight access policy, storage configuration, scan status, encryption or key-management dependencies, and recovery evidence. Never place private material in logs, issue reports, fixtures, screenshots, or documentation. If material is exposed, halt sharing and follow [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).

Historical designs and rehearsals are indexed in [engineering records](../../Development_Docs/README.md).
